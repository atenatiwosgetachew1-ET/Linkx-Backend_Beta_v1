Cleanup Validation

  • Manual enqueue succeeded for `session_tree`, `neo4j_session`, `metadata_prune`, and `artifacts_expired`.
  • Worker logs show scheduled cleanup jobs finishing successfully.
  • `window` cleanup proved managed-secret Neo4j credential resolution works.
  • Sustained cleanup burst-drain ceiling is not yet deeply benchmarked.
Network posture remained tight: `7687` limited to Servers 1, 3, and 4; `7474` limited to the admin workstation.
